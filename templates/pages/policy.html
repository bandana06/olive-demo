Privacy Policy

Welcome to Olive Cafe. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we look after your personal data when you visit our website (regardless of where you visit it from) and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

1. Important Information & Who We Are

This privacy policy helps you understand how Olive Cafe collects and processes your personal data through your use of this website, including any data you may provide through our WhatsApp Ordering system or when subscribing to our Push Notifications.

2. The Data We Collect About You

We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you which we have grouped together as follows:

  • Identity Data: includes first name, last name, username or similar identifier.
  • Contact Data: includes billing address, delivery address, email address and telephone numbers.
  • Technical Data: includes your browser type, time zone setting, operating system, and data required to send PWA Push Notifications.

3. Ordering & Table Booking

When you use our online ordering system, your cart details and contact information are temporarily structured on our local servers and then forwarded securely to our WhatsApp business account to complete the transaction. We do not store payment or credit card details on our web servers.
